A Christmas giving mission

Every child deserves to learn

This Christmas, many children in Sri Lanka will walk to school without the basics. Some carry their books in plastic bags. Some families cannot afford a school bag, let alone the monthly cost of keeping a child in school — and many of these families include our very own church children.

But a child’s future can change with one act of kindness, and it starts with you.

As we enter the season of giving, we invite you to join our 2026 Christmas fundraiser, a mission dedicated to education for children with needs.

“Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.”PROVERBS 22:6
